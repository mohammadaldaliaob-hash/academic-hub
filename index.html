💊 "Bug vs. Drug" Antibiotic Ladder

Master antimicrobial coverage from narrow spectrum to heavy guns.

Tier 1: Penicillins

Amoxicillin, Ampicillin, Penicillin G/V

Covers: Strep pharyngitis, Enterococcus, Syphilis, Listeria.

Tier 2: Cephalosporins (1st - 4th Gen)

Cefazolin (1st) ➔ Ceftriaxone (3rd) ➔ Cefepime (4th - Pseudomonas)

Covers: Surgical prophylaxis, Meningitis, Community Pneumonia, Pyelonephritis.

Tier 3: The Heavy Guns (MRSA & Pseudomonas)

Vancomycin (MRSA), Pip-Tazo / Meropenem (Pseudomonas + Anaerobes)

Covers: Hospital-acquired pneumonia, Sepsis, Febrile Neutropenia.
